Which rates does this use?

Social contributions: 20.5% on the income basis up to about €75,652, then 14.16% up to about €111,491, nothing above that, plus a 3.5% social insurance fund management fee. Hoofdberoep pays on a minimum basis of about €18,145 even when earning less; bijberoep has no minimum and owes nothing below about €1,865.

Income tax: 25% / 40% / 45% / 50% brackets at €15,820, €27,920 and €48,320, a tax-free allowance of €10,570 credited at the bottom bracket, and a 7% municipal surcharge (this varies by municipality). Social contributions are deducted before tax.

These figures are approximate and indexed yearly by RSVZ/INASTI and FOD Financiën. They have not been verified against the current income year — see the comments in tax.js.
